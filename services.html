Scope of work

Servicing and installation, split down the middle.

Our registered objects cover two kinds of work: keeping existing equipment running, and putting new systems in. Most jobs are one or the other — some are both.

Servicing & maintenance

  • Domestic, commercial and industrial air conditioning units
  • Central air conditioning plants
  • Refrigerators and freezers
  • Cooling appliances of all sizes
  • Overhauling and reconditioning of ageing equipment

Installation & commissioning

  • Air conditioning systems, domestic through industrial
  • Cold storage equipment
  • Refrigeration plants
  • Walk-in coolers and bottle coolers
  • Ice-candy plants and deep freezers
